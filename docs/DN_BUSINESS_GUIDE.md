# DN — Your App and Improvement Checklist

Current release: **26 October 2026 · Version 1.0.33 is published** at [DN](https://dn-10.web.app).

This update keeps photos beside their Moment or Activity, orders Moment stories from oldest to newest, adds a personalized daily Dashboard with independent Show/Hide controls, makes Activity lists more compact and replaces the previous filters with one reusable condition builder. Phone, tablet and desktop browser checks passed. Hands-on checks on physical phones and real signed-in account journeys remain outstanding.

## 1. What DN helps you do

DN brings your important life moments, plans, memories and money records into one place. It helps you answer:

- What is coming up?
- What do I need to do for this Moment?
- What happened, and what do I want to remember?
- How much did I earn or spend, and what was it for?

A **Moment** is the main occasion or story: a birthday, anniversary, family celebration, personal journey or diary entry. An **Activity** is a step within that Moment. A **Transaction** records income or an expense and can be connected to a Moment or Activity.

For example, a family celebration can contain Activities for booking a venue and arranging food, with the related expenses attached to each Activity. Memories and notes keep the story alongside the planning.

## 3. Rules that affect what you see

- **Filters:** The same builder is used for Moments, Activities and transaction histories. Available choices follow the records in the current account or Guest. Previous choices are kept as removable conditions; Clear all removes them. Activity progress follows the matching results and leaves cancelled work out of completion totals.

- **Upcoming Moments:** covers today through the next 30 days. Archived or cancelled Moments are excluded. A Moment type with Show on Dashboard turned off is also excluded.
- **On This Day:** shows the original recorded date from a previous year, newest first. A repeating birthday due today can also appear in Upcoming. Upcoming Activities shows pending work; completed activities can still appear as memories in On This Day.
- **Moment progress:** all remaining Activities must be complete, with at least one completed Activity, for the Moment to become Completed. No Activities does not mean Completed. Cancelled Activities do not count towards progress.
- **Moment money totals:** the Moment’s Transactions section shows all its income, expenses and balance. Search and filters narrow the history without changing these full-Moment totals. The records are reused from existing finance; no extra copies are made.
- **Monthly money totals:** Income, Expense and Balance follow the date conditions in the builder. Other conditions and search narrow the history without changing these totals. A date choice joined with an unrestricted OR choice leaves dates unrestricted. Balance means income minus expenses; it is not a bank balance.
- **Transaction order:** newest first. Every transaction needs a title, category, date and amount greater than zero.
- **Saved filters:** choices stay in that browser until you reset them. They are separate for each account or Guest. The overall Activities list, each Moment’s Activities and its Transactions remember separate choices. They do not automatically follow you to another device.
- **Original photos:** the first supported linked image is the default cover; choose another or reposition it without changing the file. These view settings stay in this browser and are not part of a backup. Unavailable originals show a fallback. The full original remains available in Attachments when stored on this device.
- **The same activities:** the overall Activities list and a Moment's Activities section show the same records. Use Activity Detail or the overall Activities list to edit; the Moment Activities tab is read-only. Each activity still belongs to one Moment. This update needs no data move or backup-format change.
- **Archiving:** keeps a Moment and its related records. Deleting a Moment or Activity does not automatically delete separately recorded transactions.
- **Repeating dates:** help show future occurrences. They do not automatically create new expenses or send reminders.

## 4. Important limits to understand

| Topic | Current position |
|---|---|
| Moving between devices | Signed-in records belong to your account, but changes are not guaranteed to appear immediately on another device. Editing the same Moment on two devices can cause one change to replace another. |
| Working without a connection | DN may open previously visited content, but dependable offline reading, saving and later transfer of every record are not guaranteed. |
| Reminders | Preferences and dates written in notes do not send a notification. An Activity currently has one date, without a separate reminder date and time. |
| Attachments | New files stay in this browser for both signed-in accounts and Guest. Accounts sync filenames and file details only. Another device needs a backup containing the originals before preview/download will work. Clearing browser site data or losing the device can lose the originals; cloud filenames cannot recover them. New saves do not use paid Firebase file storage. Older file details still cannot recreate a missing original. |
| Guest use | Guest records and original files stay in the current browser until backed up or successfully transferred at sign-in. Sign-in copies originals into a separate local account space before transferring record details; a failed copy keeps the Guest copies. Keep a backup before changing browsers or devices. |
| Backup and restore | Stored original files are included once and checked before recovery. The complete backup must fit within 50 MB; an oversized or incomplete backup is not downloaded. Older backups may contain file details only. A restore preserves existing records and adds missing information; an interruption may leave part completed. Keep backups private. |
| Removing files | Removing an attachment removes its link. Unused originals are retained; automatic file cleanup is not available. Clear Local Data removes local records and choices but does not remove stored original file objects or account files. |
| Financial detail | Recorded expenses do not have a separate paid/unpaid balance. Currency preferences do not convert amounts between currencies. |
| Phone experience | Phone-sized browser checks covered writing with visible formatting, keyboard bold/undo, compact controls and original photo previews after reopening the app. Actual phone keyboards, touch text selection, gestures and the installed-app experience still need hands-on confirmation. |

## 5. Future improvement checklist

**These are suggestions for your review. They are not completed features, approved work or delivery commitments.** “Improve an existing feature” and “Add a new feature” are kept separate below.

### Priority 1 — Trust your records

- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Consistent information across devices.** A change on one device should appear promptly on another. Decide how to handle two people or devices editing the same information before work begins. This remains a separate discussion requiring your approval.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Clear saving status.** Make it easy to see whether a change is saved, waiting for a connection or needs attention. Success means you never have to guess whether an entry was retained.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Dependable use without a connection.** Agree what can be viewed and added offline, and how you will know all changes have safely reached your account afterwards.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Stronger backup and recovery.** Make interrupted restores easier to understand and continue, review duplicates before adding records, and check that expenses remain connected to the right Activities.
- [ ] **Add a new feature — Recovery for accidental deletion.** Consider a recoverable deleted-items area and decide how long records should remain available.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Privacy and account separation.** Confirm through practical checks that each person can access only their own records and understand what is included when downloading or sharing information.

### Priority 2 — Make daily use easier

- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Finish phone testing.** Check iPhone and Android keyboards, selecting text for styles, long notes, gestures, dropdowns, scrolling, Save/Cancel and both appearances. Success means important controls remain reachable while typing.
- [ ] **Add a new feature — Separate reminders from Activity dates.** Keep the date something happened while choosing a different reminder date, time or yearly reminder. Agree how reminders should arrive and how missed reminders should be handled.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Verify local photo recovery on phones.** Confirm saving, previews, download and restoring a backup on real devices. Cloud file synchronization is outside the current device-local approach; agree a separate plan before adding it.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Clearer shared terminology.** Use consistent Moment, Activity, Memory and Transaction wording throughout the app so each action is easy to understand.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Easier reading and control.** Check text size, contrast, touch controls and use with assistance features, including on smaller phones.

### Priority 3 — Add useful planning depth

- [ ] **Add a new feature — Planned costs and unpaid balances.** Separate estimates, advances, payments and remaining balances so a future commitment is not mistaken for money already spent.
- [ ] **Add a new feature — Moment budgets.** Set a budget, compare it with recorded spending and see what remains. Agree how planned and paid costs should affect it.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Clearer downloadable reports.** Decide which summaries you need for printing or sharing, and which details should be included or hidden. Do not treat a report as a recovery backup.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — More useful Insights.** Choose the questions that matter most, such as spending by Moment, month-to-month changes or upcoming commitments, before adding more charts.
- [ ] **Improve an existing feature — Faster everyday use.** Measure how quickly DN opens and handles longer histories, then improve the slowest common tasks.

## 6. How to choose what comes next

Review each proposed improvement using this short checklist:

- [ ] What problem will it solve for me?
- [ ] Is it protecting records, improving everyday use or adding a new capability?
- [ ] What should happen in a real example?
- [ ] What should happen without a connection or if something goes wrong?
- [ ] How will I confirm the result on my phone and computer?
- [ ] Have I approved the scope before development begins?

Recommended order: **protect records first, finish reliable phone use and reminders next, then add deeper planning and reporting.**
